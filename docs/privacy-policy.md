# Privacy Policy for Halo Run

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

Halo Run does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking** of any kind.
- Your scores, unlocks, settings and stardust are stored only on your device. They may be included in your Android device backup, which Google manages under its own policy.
- **Purchases** are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Clips** you choose to share are created on your device and only leave it through the Android share sheet when you tap Share.
- The game requests only the permissions it needs: internet access (for Google Play Billing), and vibration (haptic feedback).

Children: the game contains no user-generated content, chat or links outside Google Play.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/camberr4/orbit-game or email the address on the Google Play listing.
