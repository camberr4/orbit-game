HALO RUN is a one-thumb game about the most satisfying thing in space: staying alive.

MAKE IT YOUR OWN

MUSIC THAT MOVES WITH YOU
Thirteen original songs, a pair for every biome and one for the final trial. The planet beats with the kick drum, and the music rises with the danger.
