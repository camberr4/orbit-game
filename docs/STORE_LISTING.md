**Full description (4000):**

HALO RUN is a one-thumb arcade game about the most satisfying thing in space: staying alive.

You are a glowing dot circling a planet. TAP to flip direction. HOLD to slow time, then DRAG toward the orbit you want to jump to, or just release on the golden glow for a SLINGSHOT boost. Dodge asteroids, comets, pulse mines, wall rings, spinning blades, lasers and gravity wells, all synced to music that builds with your combo.

IT KEEPS CHANGING
- Whole planets drift past. Jump from your outer orbit to theirs and they become your new home: the camera zooms in, your old planet drifts away, and the new planet's type changes the rules (breathing orbits, slippery ice, night, storms, lava) and boosts your score
- Rare cosmic events: meteor showers, solar flares, gas giants with gravity assists, black holes that bend your orbit, supernovas you survive by hiding in the planet's shadow
- Every new element is introduced one at a time with a short explanation, and collected in your Field Guide
- Speed builds in milestones, with small rewards for everything done well: near misses, razor-thin passes, chains, clutch flips, perfect slingshots

ONE RUN, TEN WORLDS
You never leave the run. Planets drift past, you jump across, and every new world changes the colours, the rules and the dangers. New planets join your pool as your best score grows, from calm gardens to the deadly dark world.

FIVE MODES AND MODIFIERS
Endless, Daily Challenge (same layout for everyone, build a streak), Time Attack, Hardcore and Zen. Stack up to two modifiers (Swift, Night Run, Heavy, Volatile, Lucky, Chill, Glass) for a bigger score multiplier.

MAKE IT YOURS
Nineteen power-ups (shield, guardian, wingman, surge, stasis, vortex, aegis and more) keep runs alive, and your score multiplier climbs with every clean move. Earn stardust, turn it into skill points and commit to one of four skill paths, holding a skill to learn it. Fly through six biomes with their own skies, music and sounds. Share a clip of your best moments. Collect badges and goals.

FAIR AND SIMPLE
- Free demo: 5 complete runs
- One-time unlock of the full game. No ads, no subscriptions, no energy timers, no other purchases
- Works fully offline. Collects no personal data
