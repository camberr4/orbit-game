HALO RUN is a one-thumb arcade game about the most satisfying thing in space: staying alive.

You are a glowing dot circling a planet. TAP to turn around. SWIPE to leap to another orbit. Dodge asteroids, comets, pulse mines, wall rings, spinning blades, laser sweeps and a growing zoo of strange creatures, while you graze danger for points and build your multiplier.

SURVIVE THE WHOLE GALAXY
Six hand-made biomes, each with its own look, soundtrack, hazards and dangers: Aurora Reach, Dust Belt, Ember Rift, Glacier Drift, Pulsar Storm and Void Maw. The longer you live, the deeper you fly. At the end of the galaxy waits a final trial that nobody forgets.

IT KEEPS CHANGING
- Whole planets drift past. Jump across and they become your new home: ten planet types, from calm gardens to the deadly dark world, each with its own rules
- Rare cosmic events: meteor showers, solar flares, black holes that bend your orbit, supernovas
- Tricks and combos: weaves, zigzags, clean laps, razor-thin passes and clutch flips all pay extra and build your multiplier
- Every new danger is explained once, and collected in your Guide

MAKE IT YOURS
More than twenty power-ups (shield, guardian, wingman, surge, stasis, vortex, aegis, bomb and more). Earn stardust, turn it into skill points and grow three skill paths: Guard, Control and Fortune. Unlock dots, trails and finishing effects. Collect achievements, with bigger prizes the harder they are.

MUSIC THAT MOVES WITH YOU
Eleven original songs, one pair for every biome plus one for the final trial. The planet beats with the kick drum, and the music changes as the danger rises.
