Privacy Policy for Halo Run

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Halo Run ("the game") is made by JellyBytes ("I", "me", "we"). You can reach me at jellybytess@gmail.com.

The short version

The game does not collect, send, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your scores, stardust, skills, unlocks and settings are saved only on your phone. I never receive them. If you use Android's own backup (Auto Backup), that data may be copied to your Google account under Google's policy, which lets you restore your progress on a new phone. You can turn that off in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold and processed entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details, your name or your card. I only learn from Google Play whether the unlock has been bought on your account, so the game can open the full galaxy. Google's own policy applies to the purchase: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

The game asks for only what it needs: internet access (so Google Play can process the purchase and confirm it) and vibration (for haptic feedback). It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over. It has no chat, no user-created content and no links outside Google Play. I do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. If you believe I have any data about you, write to me and I will answer promptly. If you are in the EU, UK or Poland you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Poland: UODO).

Changes

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date above before the change reaches you.
